# Global Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes the Global Superstore dataset to understand sales performance, profitability, market performance, geographic trends, product performance, and customer behavior.

The project includes data preprocessing using Excel and an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to generate meaningful business insights and support data-driven decision-making.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profit, and quantity performance
- Identify high-performing markets, countries, and regions
- Analyze sales and profit across customer segments
- Identify top-performing products and sub-categories
- Analyze customer sales performance
- Understand monthly sales trends
- Generate business insights and recommendations

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** – Data cleaning and preprocessing
- **Power Query** – Data transformation
- **Microsoft Power BI** – Data visualization and dashboard development
- **DAX** – Calculations and measures

## 🧹 Data Preprocessing

The dataset was reviewed and prepared before building the Power BI dashboard.

Key preprocessing activities included:

- Checking for duplicate records
- Checking missing values
- Validating data types
- Checking sales, quantity, discount, and profit values
- Creating a Profit Margin calculation
- Preparing the dataset for Power BI analysis

### 4. Insights & Recommendations

Summarizes the major findings from the analysis and provides recommendations based on sales, profitability, geographic performance, product performance, and customer segments.

---

## 💡 Key Findings

- Total sales were approximately **$12.64M**
- Total profit was approximately **$1.47M**
- The **Consumer segment** generated the highest sales and profit contribution.
- **Asia Pacific** recorded the highest sales among the markets.
- **Europe** generated the highest profit among the markets.
- The **United States** was the leading country by sales and profit.
- **Phones and Copiers** were among the strongest-performing sub-categories.
- Sales showed noticeable variation across different months.

---

## 🎯 Business Recommendations

- Focus on high-performing markets and countries to maintain growth.
- Strengthen strategies for markets with strong profitability.
- Prioritize high-performing product categories and sub-categories.
- Develop targeted strategies for the Consumer segment.
- Monitor regional and product-level profitability to improve overall business performance.
